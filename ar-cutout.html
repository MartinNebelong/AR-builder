Paper Cutouts

AR reality editor · Krea Nano Banana 2

Walk around a room. Drag rectangles over real objects — each becomes a flat paper cutout hanging in space, textured with the live camera view behind it. Tap a cutout, type what it should become, and the AI repaints it in place.

AR needs Chrome on Android, served over HTTPS (GitHub Pages), with the camera-access WebXR feature. Desktop Test runs the same select → capture → render pipeline on a webcam.
